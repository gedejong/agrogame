    ADR-018: Continuous simulation — no automatic season end¶

    Status¶

    Accepted, 2026-11-18. Edwin decided on 2026-11-17 that the game simulates continuously (#531), and on 2026-11-18 signed this ADR as drafted in the #531 refinement, with decisions D1 to D4 taken as recommended (ruling on #531). Amends ADR-017 and ADR-003; reaffirms ADR-001 rule 5.

    The children of #531 implement it one at a time (see "Decisions and provenance"). Until each lands, the code on main keeps the season behaviour it replaces.

    Context¶

    The engine began as a season-as-experiment simulator: run one season, read the yield. The game inherited that model:

    • season_complete = MATURE(first patch) or weather exhausted. When the first patch matured, the season completed for every patch, and slower crops were reset unsold at the next rollover.
    • A per-season weather window: 200 days on /step, 150 on the client's Run Season. The two series differed.
    • /start-season and the /step rollover reset crops, the season ledger and alert latches, and re-charged seed.
    • GET /report sold standing grain: first field only, at the first patch's price. The client calls it after every Harvest, so harvesting one patch sold the others mid-season.
    • PR #529 (#518) would have auto-sold every crop at completion and refused Harvest afterwards.

    In the game a season is not an experiment. Edwin (2026-11-17): "we just simulate continuously."

    Decision¶

    1. The calendar runs continuously. /step advances days; nothing completes, resets or settles on its own. There is no season number, season phase, season length or season result.
    2. Crops have a per-patch lifecycle. A crop is sown, either at game creation for the scenario's crop or by Plant. It grows, matures, and is harvested by the player. Harvest finalises the crop in the domain layer (crop history, legume N credit) and leaves the patch bare, as reset_crop(None) does. Plant requires a bare patch.
    3. Run steps up to 200 days with stop_on_pause=true. It stops on an interrupting alert: drought or nitrogen (ADR-017), harvest ready, or, on a bare farm, sowing time. Next Day and FF7 are unchanged.
    4. Harvest ready is an interrupting alert, sent once per patch per crop cycle, on the first day the patch's crop is at MATURITY. It obeys ADR-017's interrupt spacing. ADR-017's rarity target applies to the stress alerts (drought, nitrogen).
    5. Standing past ready costs yield. A crop-specific, cited loss grows with days since harvest ready, after a grace period, and is applied to the sold quantity at Harvest. The simulated grain pool is not changed, and nothing is ever sold, harvested or destroyed automatically.
    6. Weather is generated on demand in 200-day chunks. Chunk k comes from seed base_seed + k, starting at start_date + 200·k days. Chunk 0 reproduces the former single-season /step series exactly. A save stores the seed, not the series (ADR-001 rule 5).
    7. Economy (amends ADR-003, Settlement):
    8. Revenue arises only at Harvest, at the quarter of the harvest date.
    9. A full-field Harvest pays each crop at its own price and product.
    10. Seed is charged at sowing, scaled by area.
    11. GET /report never settles.
    12. Ledger entries are dated, and P&L is reported per calendar year and per harvest.
    13. There is no season reset.
    14. Alerts (amends ADR-017):
    15. Latches and spacing persist.
    16. A field's latches re-arm on Plant in that field.
    17. Day numbers count from game start.
    18. The alert log and pause_count cover the game so far.
    19. Session and save carry no season state. Legacy v1 keys are ignored on load. No player save existed when this was decided, so no migration is needed.
    20. Out of scope: the engine's season-scoped validation harness (SimulationEngine.run_season, tests/integration/test_realism.py). A season as an experiment remains correct there.

    Decisions and provenance¶

    Edwin's ruling on #531 (2026-11-18, relayed by the manager) took every recommendation of the refinement:

    • D1 (a), "Run until something needs you". Decision 3. The cap is 200 days because /step returns a snapshot per day, and the button is renamed Run.
    • D2 (a), a cited, crop-specific harvest-delay loss applied at Harvest. Decision 5. The abstracts support a grace period and then a rising loss with very different shapes per crop (References), but give no per-week rate. The rates must come from the full-text tables, and a sorghum source is still missing, so #594 is blocked on that extraction and this ADR fixes no curve.
    • D3, a P&L per calendar year plus a receipt per harvest. Decision 7.
    • D4, Plant on a standing crop is refused with a 400 ("harvest first"). Decision 2. Harvesting an immature crop stays allowed, as the salvage path.
    • D5, sign this ADR as drafted.
    • E1, NL maize does not reach maturity in the year it is sown, is a calibration question outside this ADR, tracked by #599 (probably through the cultivar layer, #469). Until it is settled, no "stalled grain fill" trigger stands in for harvest ready, and Harvest is always available.

    Implementation, by the children of #531 and two related issues. C2 (#537), C3 (#538) and #525 had merged when this ADR was accepted.

    Consequences¶

    Positive - No crop is reset unsold, and no crop is sold without the player. - Every crop leaves through Harvest, so crop history and legume N credits always apply. #518's adjacent finding dissolves. - One weather length and one seed path. - The player chooses sowing dates (for example, winter wheat in autumn, #493). - Harvest timing becomes a real decision, and grain storage (#473) fits naturally.

    Negative - About 45 API tests assumed seasons. They are deleted with replacements or adapted, child by child. - Crops that do not mature in the year sown become visible. NL maize, as calibrated, matures in May of the following year (E1 in #531, tracked by #599). - The model now meets long standing and fallow periods. Roots grow after maturity, there is no establishment failure for out-of-season sowing, and no residue is returned at harvest. Each is a follow-up. - Run returns daily snapshots: about 16 MB per 200 days on three patches. This is the reason for the cap.

    Supersedes: - ADR-017's "/start-season only sets up" and its season-start alert reset; - ADR-003's "Economy settles at the end of each season", and the #371 note that /report settles standing grain.

    Alternatives Considered¶

    • Settle automatically at season completion (#518/#529). Rejected by Edwin. It keeps the experiment boundary and refuses Harvest after it.
    • Auto-harvest at maturity. Rejected: it removes the timing decision.
    • One season per calendar year. Rejected: it is still an artificial boundary.
    • A hard harvest deadline (the crop is destroyed after N days). Rejected: the cited literature shows gradual, crop-specific losses.
    • 365-day weather chunks. Rejected: they would change the extremes inside days 1-200 and invalidate ADR-017's measured alert table.
